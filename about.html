A bit about me...

I am 25 years old and from South London, specifically, Croydon (unfortunately). This is my first time ever making my own website using purely HTML code and I was ultimately tasked with this by the guys at Generation UK & Ireland to help understand how exactly CI/CD pipelines work, and to provide some useful overall experience with web design and the like. Although the nature/content of the website was entirely up to us, I thought I'd use it to give a glimpse into my life and to assist any future employers with getting to know me better, and to have a personal project as a visual representation of my hardwork and experimentation. The keyword being experimentation as, honestly, I am winging half of the stuff with this website. But that's the whole point of it, right? You live and you learn!
